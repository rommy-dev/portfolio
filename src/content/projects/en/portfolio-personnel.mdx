## Solution

- Project pages generated from MDX files: the frontmatter holds the data (description, technologies, figures and their sources, screenshots), the body holds the text.
- Contact form sent through Resend, with per-IP rate limiting and HTML escaping.
- Dark theme, Framer Motion animations.
- Performance: hero entrance animations in CSS (the text shows up without waiting for JavaScript), images served as AVIF or WebP at the right size, a lightweight build of Framer Motion, non-interactive sections rendered on the server.
